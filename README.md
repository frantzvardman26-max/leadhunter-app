# Lead Hunter

Homeowner leads for roofing, window and home-improvement sales reps in Texas, built from public county property records and hail reports.

- **A fresh list every morning:** pick cities and ZIP codes, home age and owner-occupied only, and Lead Hunter builds the day's list from county appraisal district records
- **Hail first:** checks recent hail reports and puts homes in hit areas at the top
- **Call smart:** Do Not Call screening, calling-hours guard (9 AM to 9 PM Texas time), callbacks, appointments with reminders, and voice notes
- **Phone numbers (optional):** connect your own Tracerfy account to look up numbers, with a daily spending cap you set
- **On the road:** a door-knock route in Google Maps and a home-screen widget with today's plan
- **Your numbers:** calls, appointments and commission this month
- English and Spanish. Your leads stay on your phone, with backup and restore.

**[Download Lead Hunter for Android](https://github.com/frantzvardman26-max/leadhunter-app/releases/latest/download/LeadHunter.apk)**

## How to install

1. Tap the download link on your Android phone.
2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or Files app.
3. Tap **Install**. Lead Hunter tells you when an update is ready.

Follow the National Do Not Call Registry, your state's telemarketing rules and local solicitation rules. Lead Hunter helps you screen and keep records; you're responsible for how you contact people.
